# Honor, Not Luxury

[简体中文](README.md)

Keep the honor of a royal title without having to live in luxury.

Independently adjust title requirements for bedrooms, daily throne rooms, apparel style, apparel quality, food, minimum expectations, and work. Each can keep the existing rules, exempt permanent colonists, or exempt everyone. By default, permanent colonists are exempt from the first six requirements; work restrictions stay unchanged. Visitors and other non-colonists keep their original rules.

Titles, honor, permits, and psycasts remain, as do **throne-room requirements for bestowing ceremonies**. Requires **RimWorld 1.6, Royalty, and Harmony**. Supports English and Simplified Chinese. Choose the scopes in the game's mod settings. It can be added to or disabled in an existing save.

I'm DingDong. This mod grew out of my own play needs: I couldn't find an existing mod close enough to the idea, so I specified what I wanted. **AI wrote the code; I reviewed it and tested it in game.** I'm sharing it with players who want the same choice.
